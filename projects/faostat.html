The context

Project overview

What I built

A multi-page agricultural dashboard covering Asian countries and regions, with production, yield, harvested area, livestock, descriptive-statistics and forecasting views.

Why I built it

To make long-term agricultural patterns easier to compare across countries, regions and commodities.

The experience

Key features

Country and regional comparisons

Charts, maps, and filters make agricultural indicators easier to compare.

Historical context

Production, yield, harvested area, and livestock views organize the historical series.

Forecast views

Rice projections appear alongside historical observations and holdout error measures.

The toolkit

Tools & technologies

Tools & Platforms

  • Google Looker Studio / Data Studio

    Interactive dashboard authoring, visual reporting and filters.

Methods & Techniques

  • Data Visualization

    Charts, maps and filters for comparing countries, regions and commodities.

  • Descriptive Statistics

    Summarizing production, yield, harvested area and livestock indicators.

  • Agricultural Data Analysis

    Comparing Asian agricultural indicators across 1961–2024.

  • Forecasting

    Presenting regional and country rice forecasts for 2025–2027.

  • Forecast Evaluation

    Comparing holdout MAE and RMSE with a naive baseline.

Data Sources

  • FAO FAOSTAT

    Crops and Livestock Products data; FAOSTAT is the data source, while Looker Studio is the dashboard tool.

The approach

How it works

  • Data Visualization
  • Descriptive Statistics
  • Agricultural Data Analysis
  • Forecasting
  • Forecast Evaluation

The dashboard organizes FAOSTAT indicators into filtered charts, maps and summary views. Its forecast pages compare regional and country rice projections with historical observations and holdout error measures.

What I learned

I learned to compare indicators using consistent definitions and to examine forecast error rather than treat a projection as certainty.

The context

Project overview

What I built

A team presentation titled “From Education to Employment: A Data Story of Youth Labor Market Trends in the Philippines,” supported by a youth and education dashboard.

Why I built it

To explore school-to-work transitions, pandemic disruption and recovery through a set of complementary youth labor-market indicators.

The experience

Key features

Complementary indicators

Employment, unemployment, labor-force participation, and NEET indicators support the story.

Selected-year trends

The dashboard places youth labor-market changes in a historical context.

Gender comparisons

Comparisons across indicators support the descriptive education-to-employment narrative.

The toolkit

Tools & technologies

Tools & Platforms

  • SAP Analytics Cloud

    Dashboard charts and a story exploring Philippine youth labor-market indicators.

  • Canva

    Design and presentation of the team’s data story.

Methods & Techniques

  • Data Visualization

    Comparing employment, unemployment, labor-force participation and NEET indicators.

  • Data Storytelling

    Connecting the dashboard evidence to an education-to-employment narrative.

  • Labor Market Analysis

    Examining youth outcomes, selected-year trends and gender differences.

Data Sources

  • ILOSTAT / YouthSTATS

    Youth labor-market indicators cited in the presentation.

  • Philippine Statistics Authority

    Published labor-market statistics and supporting contextual references.

The approach

How it works

  • Data Visualization
  • Data Storytelling
  • Labor Market Analysis

The team organized ILOSTAT indicators and PSA references into dashboard comparisons and a Canva presentation. Selected-year trends and gender comparisons support the descriptive narrative.

What I learned

I learned to interpret several labor-market indicators together and to distinguish descriptive patterns from evidence of cause and effect.

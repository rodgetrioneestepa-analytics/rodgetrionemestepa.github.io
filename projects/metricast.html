The context

Project overview

What I built

An academic restaurant analytics workspace covering POS imports, performance dashboards, customer and product views, operational reports, and sales forecasts.

Why I built it

To bring related restaurant records into a clearer view of performance and support sales and demand planning.

The experience

Key features

Related POS imports

Transaction, product-sales, and payment records are brought into the same workspace.

Performance views

Sales, product, customer, and operational views organize the reference information.

Forecast review

Forecast charts and error measures support a more careful interpretation of predictions.

The toolkit

Tools & technologies

Tools & Platforms

  • Python

    Sales forecasting and data analysis.

  • CSV / POS Data Processing

    Importing and preparing related restaurant transaction records.

Methods & Techniques

  • Time-Series Forecasting

    Reviewing future sales, transaction and demand trends.

  • Forecast Evaluation

    Interpreting holdout results with MAPE and RMSE.

  • Data Visualization

    Presenting sales, product and operational patterns.

  • Database Design

    Organizing relationships between sales, line items and payment records.

Data Sources

  • Restaurant POS exports

    Transactions.csv, ProductSales.csv and Payments.csv supply related business records.

The approach

How it works

  • Time-Series Forecasting
  • Forecast Evaluation
  • Data Visualization
  • Database Design

The reference screens show a workflow for importing the three related POS files, checking data relationships, exploring performance views and reviewing actual-versus-forecast charts with error measures.

What I learned

I learned to connect related POS records and to review forecast error alongside the chart before interpreting a prediction.
